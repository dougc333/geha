## · How an HRA differs

<!-- image omitted; see source PDF -->

If you don't qualify for an HSA when you enroll in this HDHP, or later become ineligible for an HSA, we will establish an HRA for you. If you are enrolled in Medicare, you are ineligible for an HSA and we will establish an HRA for you. You must tell us if you become ineligible to contribute to an HSA.

Please review the chart at the beginning of this section, which details the differences between an HRA and an HSA. The major differences are:

-  You cannot make contributions to an HRA;
-  Funds in your HRA are forfeited if you leave the HDHP;
-  An HRA does not earn interest; and
-  HRAs can only be used to pay for qualified medical expenses, such as deductibles and coinsurance expenses, for individuals covered by the HDHP. FEHB law does not permit qualified medical expenses to include services, drugs, or supplies related to abortions; except when the life of the mother would be endangered if the fetus were carried to term, or when the pregnancy is the result of an act of rape or incest.
