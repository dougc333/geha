- G.E.H.A was founded in 1937 as the Railway Mail Hospital Association. For over 88 years, G.E.H.A has provided health insurance benefits to Federal employees and retirees.
- G.E.H.A is incorporated as a General Not-For-Profit Corporation pursuant to Chapter 355 of the Revised Statutes of the State of Missouri.

We will keep your medical and claims records confidential. Please note that we may disclose your medical and claims information (including your prescription drug utilization) to any of your treating physicians or dispensing pharmacies.
