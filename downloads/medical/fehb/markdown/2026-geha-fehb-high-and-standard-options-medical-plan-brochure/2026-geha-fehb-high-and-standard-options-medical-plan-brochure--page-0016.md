## Section 2. Changes for 2026

Do not rely only on these change descriptions; this Section is not an official statement of benefits. For that, go to Section 5, Benefits. Also, we edited and clarified language throughout the brochure; any language change not shown here is a clarification that does not change benefits.

## Changes to both High and Standard Option

- Your share of the premium rate will increase for Self Only, Self Plus One and Self and Family.  See 2026 Rates Information on the back cover of this brochure.

## Deductible:

- In-Network: The Plan in-network deductible is $500 Self and $1,000 Self Plus One or Self and Family. Previously, it was $350 for Self and $700 for Self Plus One or Self and Family.
- Out-of-Network: The Plan out-of-network deductible is $2,000 Self and $4,000 Self Plus One or Self and Family. Previously, it was $700 Self and $1,400 Self Plus One or Self and Family.

## Out-of-Pocket Maximum:

## · High Option

- In-Network: The Plan(s) in-network out-of-pocket maximum is $7,500 for Self and $15,000 for Self Plus One or Self and Family.  Previously, it was $6,000 for Self and $12,000 for Self Plus One or Self and Family.
- Out-of-Network: The Plan(s) out-of-network out-of-pocket maximum is $20,000 for Self and $40,000 for Self Plus One or Self and Family.  Previously, it was $9,000 for Self and $18,000 for Self Plus One or Self and Family.

## · Standard Option

- In-Network: The Plan(s) in-network out-of-pocket maximum is $8,000 for Self and $16,000 for Self Plus One or Self and Family.  Previously, it was $6,500 for Self and $13,000 for Self Plus One or Self and Family.
- Out-of-Network: The Plan(s) out-of-network out-of-pocket maximum is $20,000 for Self and $40,000 for Self Plus One or Self and Family.  Previously, it was $8,500 for Self and $17,000 for Self Plus One or Self and Family.

## Physician Services

## · High Option

- In-Network: The physician copays have changed for primary care, including chiropractor, mental health and substance abuse ($30 from $20), specialist and associated specialties and Urgent care ($45 from $30). The MinuteClinic ®  copay is now $15 from $10.
- Out-of-Network: The Plan's out-of-network physician cost share remains subject to deductible and coinsurance.

## · Standard Option

- In-Network: The physician copays have changed for primary care, including mental health and substance abuse ($35 from $20), specialist and associated specialties and Urgent care ($50 from $35). The MinuteClinic ®  copay is now $15 from $10.
- Out-of-Network: The Plan's out-of-network physician cost share remains subject to deductible and coinsurance.

Sex Trait-Modification: Medical, surgical and prescription drug services related to Sex-Trait Modification for diagnosed gender dysphoria, are no longer covered by the Plan. See Section 3, 5(b), and 5(f). The Plan will continue to provide benefits related to mental and behavioral health services. See Section 5(e).

Breast Screening: The age to obtain appropriate age and medical history breast screening has been lowered to 40-74. Previously, it was age 50-74.

Mental Health &amp; Substance Abuse visit: The copay for the first follow-up visit with your Physician within 30 days of an Emergency Room visit or Inpatient confinement for a Mental Health or Substance Abuse disorder is waived.
