## · Standard Option:

- The in-network coinsurance amount is 25% of the Plan allowance, after deductible. Previously it was 15% of the Plan allowance, after deductible.
- The out-of-network coinsurance amount is 50%, after deductible. Previously it was 30% of the Plan allowance, after deductible.

## Diagnostic Tests:

## · High Option

:

- In-network lab tests are $10 copay. Previously, you paid Nothing.
- In-network tests, such as but not limited to x-rays, and MRI remain subject to deductible &amp; coinsurance.

## · Standard Option

:

- In-network lab tests are subject to your deductible and coinsurance. Previously, you paid only the coinsurance, no deductible.
- You may still continue to access a QuestSelect Lab for no cost for covered lab services.
- In-network tests, such as but not limited to x-rays, non-routine mammograms, or Ultrasound are subject to Deductible &amp; Coinsurance.
- In-network tests, such as but not limited to CT, MRI and PET studies have a $250 copay, whether they are done in an office or hospital. Previously, they applied a $100 copay for physician services and a $150 copay for facilities depending on where you obtained your test.
- Out-of-network services remain subject to deductible and coinsurance for both the High and Standard Option.

- Language describing how services are covered has been edited throughout the Brochure.
