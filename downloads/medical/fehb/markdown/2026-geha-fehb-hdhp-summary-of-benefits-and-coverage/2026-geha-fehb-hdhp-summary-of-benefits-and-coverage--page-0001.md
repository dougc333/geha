Coverage for: Self Only, Self Plus One or Self and Family

|

Plan Type: HDHP

The Summary of Benefits and Coverage (SBC) document will help you choose a health Plan. The SBC shows you how you and the Plan would share the cost for covered healthcare services. NOTE: Information about the cost of this Plan (called the premium) will be provided separately. This is only a summary. Please read the FEHB Plan brochure (RI 71-014) that contains the complete terms of this Plan. All benefits are subject to the definitions, limitations, and exclusions set forth in the FEHB Plan brochure . Benefits may vary if you have other coverage, such as Medicare. For general definitions of common terms, such as allowed amount, balance billing, coinsurance, copayment, deductible, provider, or other underlined terms see the Glossary. You can get the FEHB Plan brochure at www.geha.com, and view the Glossary at www.healthcare.gov/sbc-glossary. You can call 800-821-6136 to request a copy of either document.
