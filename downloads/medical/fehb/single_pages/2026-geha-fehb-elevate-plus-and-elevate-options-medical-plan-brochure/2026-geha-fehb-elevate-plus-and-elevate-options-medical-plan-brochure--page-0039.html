Obesity counseling, screening, and referral for those persons at or

above the USPSTF obesity prevention risk factor level, to

intensive nutrition and behavioral weight-loss therapy,

and B recommendations are covered as part of prevention and

treatment of obesity as follows:

• Nutrition and behavioral weight-loss counseling therapy in

children and adolescents age 6 years or older with BMI greater

than or equal to 95th percentile on CDC growth charts for age

and sex.

• Family centered programs when medically identified to

support obesity prevention and management by an in-network

provider in children and adolescents age 6 years or older with

BMI greater than or equal to 95th percentile on CDC growth

charts for age and sex.

• Obesity screening for those persons below the USPSTF

obesity prevention risk factor.
