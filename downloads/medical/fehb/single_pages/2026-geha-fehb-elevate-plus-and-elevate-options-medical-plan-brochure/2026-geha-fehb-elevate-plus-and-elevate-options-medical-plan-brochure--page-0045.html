Note: Growth hormone therapy (GHT) is covered under the

prescription drug benefit. We only cover GHT when we

preauthorize the treatment. Call 800-821-6136 for

preauthorization. We will ask you to submit information that

establishes GHT is medically necessary. Ask us to authorize GHT

before you begin treatment; otherwise, we will only cover GHT

services from the date you submit the information. If you do not

ask or if we determine GHT is not medically necessary, we will

not cover the GHT or related services and supplies. See Other

services that require preauthorization in Section 3.

rehabilitation). See Section 5(c), Outpatient hospital
