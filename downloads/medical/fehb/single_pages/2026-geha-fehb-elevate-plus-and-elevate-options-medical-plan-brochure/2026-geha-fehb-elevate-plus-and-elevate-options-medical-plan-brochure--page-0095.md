## Elevate Plus and Elevate Options

| Member Portal                     | Your family's healthcare resources, in your hands whether at home or on the go. The online member portal provides instant access to your family's critical health information - anytime and anywhere. Whether you want to find a physician near you, check the status of a claim or speak directly with a healthcare professional, the portal is your go-to resource. Key features include: • Search for physicians or facilities by location or specialty • Store favorite physicians and facilities • View and share health plan ID card information • Access the Plan's telehealth administrative partner at geha.com/Telehealth-Visit • Check reward program status and activities • Can be personalized with individual member avatar, notes and reminders • Complete confidentiality • Check health-related financial account balance • Locate nearby convenience clinics, urgent care facilities, and emergency rooms • Check status of deductible and out-of-pocket spending For more information, visit geha.com/My-Member-Portal. |
| Weight Management                 | G.E.H.A offers a number of services and tools for weight management. • BMI calculation through on-line health risk assessment • Nutrition counseling (see Educational Classes and Programs, Section 5(a)) • Behavioral change programs with coaching for members who qualify • Discounts for gym memberships and other services through Connection Fitness ®                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

| Clinical Assistance Programs      | G.E.H.A offers several programs to support and partner with you during your healthcare journey. When you are experiencing a new diagnosis or struggling with managing your health, G.E.H.A is here to support you by offering various programs. You may find out more about our programs at geha.com/Your-Health-Journey. A nurse case manager may contact you to participate in one of these programs and provide assistance with the coordination of your care, education and clinical support. Please note that eligibility for these services may vary, and not all members will qualify.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
