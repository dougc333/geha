## HDHP

|                              | Note: If your effective date in the HDHP is after the 1st of the month, the earliest your HSA will be established is the 1st of the following month. If you are new to this Plan based on an Open Season change, your first premium pass through will be made available no earlier than February as new enrollees and terminations from Open Season are still being received in January. Your last HSA pass-through contribution for the current plan year will fund in January of the following plan year as long as you maintain eligibility through the end of the current plan year. For 2026, a monthly premium pass through that is not a whole number will have any remaining balance included in the final monthly contribution. In addition, you may establish pre-tax HSA deductions from your paycheck to fund your HSA up to IRS limits using the same method that you use to establish other deductions (i.e., Employee Express, MyPay, etc.).   |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
