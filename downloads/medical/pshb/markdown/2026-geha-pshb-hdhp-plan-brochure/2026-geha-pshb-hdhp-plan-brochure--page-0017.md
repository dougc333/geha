## Section 2. New for 2026

Do not rely only on these change descriptions; this Section is not an official statement of benefits.  For that, go to Section 5, Benefits.  Also, we edited and clarified language throughout the brochure; any language change not shown here is a clarification that does not change benefits.

## Changes to our High Deductible Health Plan

- Your share of the premium rate will increase for Self Only, Self Plus One and Self and Family, see 2026 Rate Information on the back page of this brochure.
- Breast Cancer Screening: The age range for preventive breast cancer screening for women is expanding to ages 40-74. Members pay no cost share for these services. See Section 5(a), Professional Services/Medical Preventive Care Adult.
- Surgical and Anesthesia Services - Reconstructive surgery: Coverage for Sex-Trait Modification for diagnosed gender dysphoria, and medications including hormone treatments is not covered. If you are mid-treatment under this Plan, for services which you received coverage under the 2025 Plan brochure, contact Customer Care at 800-821-6136. See Section 3, 5(b): Surgical and Anesthesia Services, or Section 5(f): Prescription Drug Benefits.
- In-Network and Out-of-Network Deductibles : The annual deductibles are increasing.
- In Network deductible - $1,800 for Self Only enrollment, $3,600 for Self Plus One enrollment, and $3,600 for Self and Family enrollments.
- Out-of-network deductible - $4,500 Self Only, $9,000 Self Plus One and $9,000 Self and Family enrollments. See Section 4.

## Changes to our PDP EGWP for the High Deductible Health Plan

EGWP Prescription Drug Maximum: The prescription drug True out-of-pocket maximum (TrOOP) will increase from $2,000 to $2,100 per person annually. See Section 5(f)(a): Prescription Drug Benefits, Section 9: Coordinating Benefits with Medicare and Other Coverage.
