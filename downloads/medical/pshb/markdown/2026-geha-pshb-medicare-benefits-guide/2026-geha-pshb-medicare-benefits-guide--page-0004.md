## ORIGINAL MEDICARE PARTS A &amp; B

This coverage is provided by the government to give annuitants baseline coverage for hospital (Part A) and medical (Part B) services. Benefits are limitfed, with beneficiaries responsible for 20% of covered hospital and medical costs.

## G.E.H.A + MEDICARE

Combine G.E.H.A's Standard, High or HDHP plans to get 100% coverage for all covered Medicare services with Medicare Part A &amp; B primary, optional Employer Group Waiver Plan (EGWP) Part D drug coverage, and valuable extras like a vision discount for glasses and contacts. 1
