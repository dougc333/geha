The contraceptive benefit includes at least one option in each of the HRSA-supported categories of contraception (as well as the screening, education, counseling, and follow-up care). Over the counter (prescription required) and prescription drugs approved by the FDA to prevent an unintended pregnancy are included. Any contraceptive that is not already available without cost sharing on the formulary can be accessed through the contraceptive exceptions process described on Our website at geha.com/Family-Planning-Options or by calling CVS Caremark at 844-443-4279. Exception requests for contraceptive coverage will be processed within 24 hours of receiving complete information.
