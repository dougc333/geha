## High and Standard Options:

- We added the following procedure codes:

- D2989 Excavation of a tooth resulting in the determination of non-restorability (Class B) - Limited to once per tooth, per lifetime
- D9224 Administration of general anesthesia with advanced airway - first 15 minute increment, or any portion thereof (Class C)
- D9225 Administration of general anesthesia with advanced airway - each subsequent 15 minute increment, or any portion thereof (Class C)
- Sedation/Anesthesia/Analgesia will be limited to a maximum of 12 total units per date of service, regardless of the combination of sedation and anesthesia codes used.
- Orthodontic payments will now be made on a monthly basis instead of a quarterly basis.

- We have added a new non-FEDVIP benefit offering. Connection Fitness® includes discounts on gym memberships, access to online tools and activity.
