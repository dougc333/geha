## You Pay:

- High Option
- In-Network: 50% of plan allowance.
- Out-of-Network: 50% of plan allowance and any difference between our allowance and the billed amount.

## · Standard Option

- In-Network: 65% of plan allowance.
- Out-of-Network: 70% of plan allowance and any difference between our allowance and the billed amount. Subject to the deductible.

## Major restorative services

D0160 Detailed and extensive oral evaluation - problem focused, by report - Detailed and extensive oral evaluations are limited to once per covered person per dentist, per lifetime.

Crowns, inlays, onlays and posts and cores are payable only when required for restorative purposes and necessary due to extensive decay or significant tooth fracture and evidence is presented showing the tooth cannot be adequately restored with an amalgam or composite restoration.
