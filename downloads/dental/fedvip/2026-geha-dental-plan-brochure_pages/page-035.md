## Adjunctive general services (cont.)

D9610 Therapeutic parenteral drug, single administration - Therapeutic drug injections are not covered if performed routinely or in conjunction with, or for the purposes of, general anesthesia, analgesia, sedation or premedication. Coverage determined by report.

D9612 Therapeutic parenteral drugs, two or more administrations, different medications - Therapeutic drug injections are not covered if performed routinely or in conjunction with, or for the purposes of, general anesthesia, analgesia, sedation or premedication. Coverage determined by report.

D9613 Infiltration of sustained release therapeutic drug, per quadrant - When covered, D9613 Infiltration of sustained release therapeutic drug, per quadrant once per date of service for members up to age 22, when submitted with extractions (D7220 Removal of impacted tooth - soft tissue, D7230 Removal of impacted tooth - partially bony, D7240 Removal of impacted tooth - completely bony, D7241 Removal of impacted tooth - completely bony, with unusual surgical complications and D7251 Coronectomy - intentional partial tooth removal, impacted teeth only) not to exceed a maximum allowable of $300 per date of service.

D9930 Treatment of complications (post-surgical) - unusual circumstances, by report - Coverage determined by report.

Cleaning and inspection of removable dentures which includes D9932 Cleaning and inspection of removable complete denture, maxillary, D9933 Cleaning and inspection of removable complete denture, mandibular, D9934 Cleaning and inspection of removable partial denture, maxillary and D9935 Cleaning and inspection of removable partial denture, mandibular are limited to once per Calendar year, not within 6 months of D1110 Prophylaxis - adult. These are considered integral if provided on the same date of service as D1110 Prophylaxis - adult.

D9941 Fabrication of athletic mouthguard - Limited to once per covered person per Calendar Year.

D9943 Occlusal guard adjustment - Limited to once per year.

Occlusal guards which includes D9944 Occlusal guard - hard appliance, full arch, D9945 Occlusal guard - soft appliance, full arch and D9946 Occlusal guard - hard appliance, partial arch are limited to once per Calendar Year for covered persons age 13 or older and treatment is for bruxism or to protect the teeth from grinding, chipping or fracture. An occlusal guard for temporomandibular joint dysfunction or other non-dental related treatment is not covered. Coverage determined by report.

D9974 Internal bleaching - per tooth - Internal bleaching of discolored teeth is covered for endodontically treated anterior teeth once per covered person per tooth every 3 calendar years. External bleaching of discolored teeth is not a covered benefit.

*D9999 Unspecified adjunctive procedure, by report - The plan allowance will be determined upon review of the report.

## Not covered:

- Precision attachments, personalization, precious metal bases, and other specialized techniques
- Replacement of dentures that have been lost, stolen or misplaced
- Removable or fixed prostheses initiated prior to the effective date of coverage or inserted/cemented after the coverage ending date
- Any exclusions or limitations listed under Section 7 of this plan document
