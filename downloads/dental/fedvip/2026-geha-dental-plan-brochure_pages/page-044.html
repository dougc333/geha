2. We have 30 days from the date we received your request to:

  • Pay the claim (or, if applicable, arrange for the dental care provider to give you the care); or
  • Write to you and maintain our denial - go to step 3; or
  • Ask you or your provider for more information. If we ask your provider, we will send you a copy of our request. You or your provider must send the information so that we receive it within 60 days of our request. We will then decide within 30 more days. If we do not receive the information within 60 days, we will decide within 30 days of the date the information was due. We will base our decision on the information we already have. We will write to you with our decision.
  • If you do not agree with our final decision, you may request an independent third party, mutually agreed upon by us and OPM, final appeal to review the decision. The decision of the independent third party is binding for us and is the final administrative review of your claim. To request an independent third-party review you must:
  • Write to us within 90 days of our letter maintaining denial; and
  • Send your request to us by logging in at www.geha.com/FEDVIPInfo and completing the online appeal submission form, by email gehadentalappeals@mygeha.com, by fax: 866-963-0156, or by mail: G.E.H.A Post Service Appeal, P.O. Box 21324, Eagan, MN 55121.

How to request an online appeal:

  1. Log in to your MyGEHA account - Visit www.geha.com/FEDVIPInfo, click the Sign in or register drop-down in the top-right corner, then will click MyGEHA member portal.
  2. Hover over Claims and EOBs drop down on top banner, under Claim Appeals- select Submit an appeal.
  3. Search for claims you'd like to appeal/inquire about by selecting member from the Select Person drop down. Apply filter and select claim number.
  4. After selecting the claim, select Appeal this claim. This link will only be visible if the claim has been processed or if the claim is appealable.
  5. After selecting Appeal this claim, users will get a pop-up box containing information on the process. Select the ineligible code you wish to have reviewed and select continue.
  6. Enter detailed information in the Claim dispute comments section and upload documents, then select continue.
  7. Add your contact information, then select continue.
  8. Review your request summary and select Submit when you're finished.
  9. Include a statement about why you are requesting an independent third-party final appeal review. This statement should include why you believe our decision to deny your claim was wrong, based on specific benefit provisions in this brochure; and include copies of documents that support your claim, such as dentist's letters, provider narratives, X-rays or other records, and explanation of benefits (EOB) forms.
