## Non-FEDVIP Benefits

## Our dental plans go beyond your oral health with these great value-added benefits and discount programs

## Connection Vision® - 877-808-8538 - geha.com/Save-Vision

Connection Vision provides eye care to you and your covered family members at low cost and is available at no extra cost to all G.E.H.A Connection Dental Federal members. Connection Vision includes low copays for eye exams and discounts on lenses, frames and contact lenses. Call or go online to find a list of participating locations.

## Connection Hearing® - 844-224-2712 - geha.com/Hearing-Savings

G.E.H.A members and their families can save 30% to 60% off the average retail price of hearing aids through the Plan's hearing partner. Shop from a selection of more than 100 of the latest hearing aids from the top hearing aid manufacturers in the world. Call to set up an appointment with a provider in your area or go online to learn more.

## Connection Fitness® - 877-434-2336 - geha.com/Fit-For-Less

G.E.H.A promotes healthy lifestyles and fitness activities. All G.E.H.A plan members can take advantage of our Connection Fitness program including discounts on gym memberships, access to online tools and activity tracking. Access to more than 22,500 nationwide participating fitness centers and more than 14,000 digital workout videos for a minimal monthly fee (plus a small, one-time enrollment fee and applicable taxes).

## Oral Care Program - 855-944-8361 - geha.com/Smile-Savings

G.E.H.A members save up to 70% off a premium electric toothbrush and 20% off of the lowest-published price for professional teeth whitening when using our preferred partner. Supplies such as custom-fitted trays, teeth whitening gel and desensitizing gel can be ordered online. Call or go online to learn more about the brands and products offered by G.E.H.A.

## Emergency Response Services - 800-640-0518 - geha.com/savings/medical-alert-system

G.E.H.A members and their families can receive discounted emergency response services. To receive this G.E.H.A discount (free activation plus a 10% discount on your monthly billing), call and request a free brochure. A representative will follow up after the brochure arrives to discuss your options.

These benefits are neither offered nor guaranteed under contract with the FEDVIP Program but are made available to all enrollees who become members of G.E.H.A and their eligible family members. Exceptions may apply. Call or go online to find out more about the program, such as verifying eligibility, program benefits and finding participating locations.
