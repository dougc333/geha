## Disputed Claims Process

If any benefits become payable to anyone who, in our opinion, is legally incapable of giving us a valid receipt or release, we may pay a portion of such benefits to any individual or institution we believe has assumed custody or principal support for such person, provided we have not received a request for payment from the person's legal guardian or other legally appointed representative.

For services you receive outside of the United States, send itemized bills that include date the services were rendered. Benefits will be calculated using the daily rate of exchange for the date of service and reimbursed in United States currency. All international claims should be submitted to G.E.H.A Dental Claims, P. O. Box 21191, Eagan, MN 55121.

Please reply promptly when we ask for additional information. We may delay processing or deny your claim if you do not respond. See Section 6 International Services and Supplies for more information.

Send us all of the documents for your claims as soon as possible. You must submit the claim by December 31 of the year after the year you receive the service, unless timely filing was prevented by administrative operations of the Government or legal incapacity, provided the claim was submitted as soon as reasonably possible. You must submit the requested information by December 31 of the year after you receive the service, for benefit determination. If submitted after this date, charges will not be considered for benefit payment. Once we pay benefits, checks are stale dated after 2 years. There is a 3year limitation on the reissuance of uncashed checks.

We may, at our option, require supporting documentation such as clinical reports, charts, radiographic images, and study models.

Follow this disputed claims process if you disagree with our decision on your claim or request for services.  The FEDVIP law does not provide a role for OPM to review disputed claims.

Disputed Claim Steps:

1. Ask us in writing to reconsider our initial decision by requesting a formal appeal. You must:

-  Write to us within 6 months from the date of our decision; and
-  Send your request to us by logging in at www.geha.com/FEDVIPInfo and completing the online appeal submission form, by email gehadentalappeals@mygeha.com, by fax: 866-963-0156, or by mail: G.E.H.A Post Service Appeal, P.O. Box 21324, Eagan, MN 55121.

How to request an online appeal:

1. Log in to your MyGEHA account - Visit www.geha.com/FEDVIPInfo, click the Sign in or register drop-down in the top-right corner, then will click MyGEHA member portal.
2. Hover over Claims and EOBs drop down on top banner, under Claim Appeals- select Submit an appeal.
3. Search for claims you'd like to appeal/inquire about by selecting member from the Select Person drop down. Apply filter and select claim number.
4. After selecting the claim, select Appeal this claim. This link will only be visible if the claim has been processed or if the claim is appealable.
5. After selecting Appeal this claim, users will get a pop-up box containing information on the process. Select the ineligible code you wish to have reviewed and select continue.
6. Enter detailed information in the Claim dispute comments section and upload documents, then select continue.
