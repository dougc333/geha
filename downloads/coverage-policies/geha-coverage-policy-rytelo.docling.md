## Indication Specific Approval Criteria:

## Myelodysplastic Syndrome (MDS)

-  Member is 18 years of age or older; AND
-  Member's MDS is classified as IPSS low- to intermediate-1 risk; AND
-  Member's absolute neutrophil count is 1.5 x 10⁹/L or greater within the last 30 days; AND
-  Member's platelets are 75 x 10⁹/L or greater within the last 30 days; AND
-  Member has no prior treatment with either lenalidomide OR hypomethylating agents; AND
-  Member does not have del(5q) cytogenic abnormality ; AND
-  Member is transfusion-dependent requiring 4 or more units of red blood cells in previous 8 weeks; AND
-  Member has ring sideroblasts (RS) of 15% or more (or 5% or more with SF3B1 mutation); AND
- o Member has serum EPO &gt; 500 mU/mL; OR
- o Member has serum EPO of 500 or less; AND
-  Member has lost response to luspatercept; OR
-  Member has RS less than 15% (or RS less than 5% with SF3B1 mutation); AND
- o Member has serum EPO greater than 500 mU/mL; AND
-  Member and no response to immunosuppressive therapy (IST), did not tolerate IST, relapsed after IST or is a poor candidate for IST; OR
- o Member has serum EPO of 500 mU/mL or less; AND
-  Member has lost response to erythropoietin-stimulating agents (ESAs); OR
-  Member has failed to respond to ESA therapy; OR

## Universal Approval Criteria:

Unless otherwise noted, the review criteria used by OncoHealth to determine medical necessity for anticancer treatments and supportive agents include, but is not limited to:

-  National Comprehensive Cancer Network® (NCCN) - use consistent with NCCN recommendations carrying a Category 1 or 2A level of evidence; OR
-  United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA) labeling - new drugs or regimens (combinations of drugs) consistent with the product labeling; OR
-  Indications not included in the official FDA labeling or recommended by NCCN (Category 1 or 2A level of evidence) may be considered if determined to be medically necessary per one or more of the following compendia:
- o Clinical Pharmacology (Strong For); OR
- o Wolters Kluwer Lexi-Drugs® (Level A); OR
-  Other uses of drugs and biologics may be considered medically necessary if supported as safe and effective according to peer-reviewed articles from one of the following journals:
- o American Journal of Medicine; Annals of Internal Medicine; Annals of Oncology; Annals of Surgical Oncology; Biology of Blood and Marrow Transplantation; Blood; Bone Marrow Transplantation; British Journal of Cancer; British Journal of Hematology; British Medical Journal; Cancer; Clinical Cancer Research; Drugs; European Journal of Cancer (formerly the European Journal of Cancer and Clinical Oncology); Gynecologic Oncology; International Journal of Radiation, Oncology, Biology, and Physics; The Journal of the American Medical Association, Journal of Clinical Oncology; Journal of the National Cancer Institute; Journal of the National Comprehensive Cancer Network (NCCN); Journal of Urology; Lancet; Lancet Oncology; Leukemia; The New England Journal of Medicine; Radiation Oncology; AND

-  Non-standard protocols may be approved based on unique clinical circumstances;
