### Indication Specific Criteria Breast cancer -- unresectable or.metastatic  

The member is 18 years of age or older; AND 

The member has an ECOG performance status of 0-11; AND 

The member has hormone receptor (HR) positive disease; AND 

The member has HER2 negative (IHC 0, IHC 1+ or IHC 2+/ISH-) disease; AND 

Datroway is used as subsequent therapy after the following:

O Endocrine Therapy; AND 

0Chemotherapy; AND 

O Enhertu (if member is eligible for treatment with Enhertu, as per current version of.NCCN Guidelines)

### EGFR-Mutated Non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer -Iocally advanced or metastatic. 

The member is 18 years of age or older; AND 

The member has an ECOG performance status of 0-12; AND 

The member has one of the following EGFR mutations: exon 19 deletion, L858R, L861Q, exon 20insertion, S7681, or G719X; AND 



Has not had prior treatment with:

O Any agent including antibody drug conjugate (ADC) containing a chemotherapeutic agent targeting topoisomerase I; OR 



O TROP2-targeted therapy 

Datroway will be used as subsequent therapy after the following:.

O Platinum-based (i.e. carboplatin, cisplatin) systemic chemotherapy; AND 

O EGFR-directed therapy (i.e. afatinib, dacomitinib, erlotinib, gefitinib, osimertinib)
