## Indication Specific Criteria:

## Biliary Tract Cancer (BTC)

Ziihera is used as a single agent; AND 

The member is 18 years of age or older; AND 

The member has an ECOG performance status of O to 2; AND 

The member has unresectable or gross residual or metastatic disease; AND 

The member has HER2-positive (IHC 3+) disease; AND 

Ziihera is used as subsequent therapy; AND 

The member did not progress on prior HER2 targeted therapy; AND 

Prescribed by, or in consultation with, an oncologist
