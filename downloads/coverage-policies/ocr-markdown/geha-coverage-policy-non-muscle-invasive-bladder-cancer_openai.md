## Inlexzo (gemcitabine)

Member is 18 years of age or older; AND.

Member has BCG-unresponsive non-muscle invasive bladder cancer with carcinoma in situ (CiS),with or without papillary tumors that has the following characteristics:

O High-grade; AND 

O Urothelial (transitional cell) histology AND 

O BCG unresponsive defined by one of the following:

Persistent or recurrent CIS within 12 months of receiving adequate BCG4; OR Recurrent high-grade Ta/T1 disease within 6 months of receiving adequate BCG5; OR 



T1 high-grade disease at the first evaluation following complete induction BCG course alone (5 of 6 doses of an initial induction course); AND.

Prescribed by, or in consultation with, a urologist or oncologist.
