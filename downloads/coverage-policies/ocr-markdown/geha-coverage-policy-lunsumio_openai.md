## Indication Specific Criteria:

## Follicular Lymphoma -- Relapsed or Refractory (Grade 1, 2 or 3A)

Lunsumio will be used as monotherapy; AND 

The member is 18 years of age or older; AND 

The member has not had prior treatment with chimeric antigen receptor T-cell (CART-T) therapy within 30 days before administration; AND 



The member has not had an allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplant; AND 

 The member has not had an autologous stem cel transplant within the past 100 days; AND 

The member's Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (EcoG) score of Oor 2; AND 

The member has no history of CNS lymphoma or CNS disorders; AND 

The member has no active infection (e.g. viral, bacterial, fungal) such as HIV, active hepatitis B or active hepatitis C; AND 



 The member has received 2 or more prior lines of therapy that includes anti-cD20 therapy (e.g.,rituximab) and an alkylating agent (e.g., cyclophosphamide); AND.



Prescribed by, or in consultation with, an oncologist.
