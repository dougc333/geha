## Indication Specific Criteria:

## Myelofibrosis (MF)

Member is 18 years of age or older; AND.

Member has intermediate or high-risk MF, including primary MF or secondary MF1 ; AND 

Member has anemia as defined by a hemoglobin &lt; 10 g/dL as measured in the last 28 days; OR 

Member has transfusion-dependent anemia.
