# Trodelvy (Sacituzumab govitecan-hziy)

Original Effective Date: 10/1/2025Revision Date(s): 10/1/2025Review Date: 10/1/2025Policy type: Medical Necessity Line of Business: All GEHA Medical Plans 

Authorizations are issued for 6 (six) months, unless the ordering physician requests a different timespan or the patients' unique circumstance or condition supports the medical necessity for a different authorization timeframe. Reauthorization requests are reviewed for efficacy, safety and tolerability.

## Indication Specific Criteria 

## Unresectable Locally Advanced or Metastatic Breast Cancer

The member is 18 years of age or older; AND.

The member has an ECOG performance status of 0-11; AND 

The member has HER2 negative (IHC 0, IHC 1+ or IHC 2+/ISH-) and hormone receptor (HR) positive disease; AND 



 The member has progressed on prior endocrine and CDK 4/6 inhibitor therapy;AND 



The member has progressed on at least two prior lines of systemic 

chemotherapy (at least one of these prior lines in the metastatic setting); AND 

The member is not a candidate for fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki (Enhertu);OR 



The member has triple negative breast cancer; AND.

The member has progressed on or after at least two prior lines of systemic therapy (at least one of these prior lines in the metastatic setting).

## Limitations 

The treatment of members with urothelial cancer with Trodelvy is not recommended.
