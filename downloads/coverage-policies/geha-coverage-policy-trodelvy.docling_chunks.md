## Chunk 3

Unresectable Locally Advanced or Metastatic Breast Cancer
- The member is 18 years of age or older; AND
- The member has an ECOG performance status of 0-1 1 ; AND
- The member has HER2 negative (IHC 0, IHC 1+ or IHC 2+/ISH-) and hormone receptor (HR) positive disease; AND
- The member has progressed on prior endocrine and CDK 4/6 inhibitor therapy; AND
- The member has progressed on at least two prior lines of systemic chemotherapy (at least one of these prior lines in the metastatic setting); AND
- The member is not a candidate for fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki (Enhertu); OR
- The member has triple negative breast cancer; AND
- The member has progressed on or after at least two prior lines of systemic therapy (at least one of these prior lines in the metastatic setting)



## Chunk 4

Limitations
- The treatment of members with urothelial cancer with Trodelvy is not recommended.
