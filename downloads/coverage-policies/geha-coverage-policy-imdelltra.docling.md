## Indication Specific Approval Criteria:

## Extensive Stage Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC)

-  Imdelltra will be given as monotherapy; AND
-  The member must be 18 years of age or older; AND
-  The member has an ECOG performance status of 0 to 1 1 ; AND
-  The member has had progression on or after platinum-based chemotherapy; AND
- o Imdelltra is being used as second line therapy for patients whose chemotherapy free interval (CTFI) is less than 6 months; OR
-  Patient has documented intolerance or contraindication to platinum based therapy; OR
- o Imdelltra is being used as third line or later therapy; OR
-  The member has ES-SCLC that has metastasized to the brain.
