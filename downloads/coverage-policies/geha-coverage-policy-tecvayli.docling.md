## Indication Specific Criteria:

## Multiple Myeloma (MM)

- Tecvayli will be given as monotherapy; AND
- The member is 18 years of age or older; AND
- The member has had no prior treatment with a B cell maturation antigen (BCMA) targeted therapy (e.g. Abecma, Carvykti, Elrexfio); AND
- The member has not had an allogeneic stem cell transplant within the past 6 months; AND
- The member has not had an autologous stem cell transplant within the past 12 weeks; AND
- The member has an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) score of 0 or 1 1 ; AND
- The member does not have CNS involvement or clinical signs of meningeal involvement of multiple myeloma; AND
- The member's prior therapy includes at least four prior systemic therapies (including a proteasome inhibitor (PI), an immunomodulatory agent (IMiD), and an anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody)

## Universal Approval Criteria:

Unless otherwise noted, the review criteria used by OncoHealth to determine medical necessity for anticancer treatments and supportive agents include, but is not limited to:

- National Comprehensive Cancer Network® (NCCN) - use consistent with NCCN recommendations carrying a Category 1 or 2A level of evidence; OR
- United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA) labeling - new drugs or regimens (combinations of drugs) consistent with all components of the product labeling; OR
- Indications not included in the official FDA labeling or recommended by NCCN (Category 1 or 2A level of evidence) may be considered if determined to be medically necessary per one or more of the following compendia:
- o Clinical Pharmacology (Strong For); OR
- o Wolters Kluwer Lexi-Drugs® (Level A); OR

1 ECOG score of 2 or 3 will be considered on a case-by-case basis.
