Laboratory Evaluations

  1. Testosterone (total and free)
  2. Thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH)
  3. Hysterosalpingogram (HSG)
  4. Diagnostic hysteroscopy
  5. Diagnostic laparoscopy with or without chromotubation
  6. Leukocyte count in semen
  7. Pelvic ultrasound (transabdominal or transvaginal)
  8. Post ejaculatory urinalysis
  9. Scrotal, testicular or transrectal ultrasound
  10. Semen analysis
  11. Sonohysterogram or saline infusion ultrasound
  12. Testicular biopsy
  13. Vasography

Note: Pre-Implantation Genetic Testing requires preauthorization. Pre-Implantation Genetic Testing Policy Link. The most reliable method is to use the provider portal or the provided phone number on the member's Insurance ID card.

Artificial Insemination

Artificial insemination [Intravaginal insemination (IVI), Intra-cervical insemination (ICI), Intrauterine insemination (IUI)] is considered medically necessary for the treatment of infertility for any of the following:

  1. Infertility with male factor fertility problems (2 or more semen analyses, measured 4 weeks apart, have 1 or more variables below the 5th percentile); or
  2. Unexplained infertility problems; or
  3. Minimal to mild endometriosis; or
  4. Medically refractory erectile dysfunction or vaginismus preventing intercourse; or
  5. HIV positive and undergoing sperm washing; or
  6. Clomiphene-citrate-stimulated artificial insemination (intra-cervical insemination or IUI) for infertile females with WHO Group II ovulation disorders such as polycystic ovarian syndrome who ovulate with clomiphene citrate but have not become pregnant after ovulation induction with clomiphene.

If a member meets appropriate clinical scenarios with supporting provider documentation, approval may be granted for IUI treatments with oral medications and triggers.

Benefits for medications related to the treatment of fertility preservation are considered under the Outpatient Prescription Drug benefit or under the Pharmaceutical Products. Check the member specific benefit plan document for inclusion or exclusion. For medication related benefit questions, please contact customer service.
