Benefit language clarification for Iatrogenic Infertility coverage: Benefits are limited to one successful cycle of fertility preservation for Iatrogenic Infertility per covered person during the entire period of time enrolled for coverage under the healthcare plan. Successful fertility preservation cycle includes: 1. 8-10 mature oocytes obtained within no more than 3 attempts of retrieval or 2. 3-4 vials of sperm obtained within no more than 3 attempts of separate sperm extraction sessions.
