Hypogammaglobulinemia in Multiple Myeloma
- Member has a documented secondary diagnosis of hypogammaglobulinemia defined as IgG <400mg/dL; OR
- Member has a history of serious or recurrent bacterial infections

Hypogammaglobulinemia in Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia
- Member has a documented secondary diagnosis of hypogammaglobulinemia defined as IgG <500mg/dL; AND
- Member has a history of recurrent bacterial infections

Management of Checkpoint Inhibitor Related Toxicities
- Member has been receiving therapy with a checkpoint inhibitor; AND
- IVIG is being used for management of the following immunotherapy-related toxicities:
- o Member is experiencing myocarditis that has not improved within 24-48 hours of starting pulse-dose methylprednisolone; OR
- o May be considered as an adjunctive therapy to rituximab for severe or life-threatening bullous dermatitis; OR
- o May be considered for Stevens-Johnson Syndrome or Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis; OR
- o May be considered for moderate or severe steroid-refractory myalgias or myositis; OR
- o Member is experiencing severe myasthenia gravis; OR
- o Being used as an adjunctive therapy to methylprednisolone for moderate or severe GuillainBarré Syndrome or severe peripheral neuropathy; OR
- o Being used in combination with methylprednisolone for severe encephalitis; OR
- o Member is experiencing transverse myelitis; OR
- o May be considered for moderate or severe pneumonitis if no improvement after 48-72 hours of corticosteroids
