Anktiva (nogapendekin alfa inbakicept-pmln)
- Member is 18 years of age or older; AND
- Member will be receiving nogapendekin alfa inbakicep-pmln (Anktiva) in combination with BCG; AND
- Member has non-muscle invasive bladder cancer with carcinoma in situ (CIS) that has the following characteristics:
- o High-grade; AND
- o Urothelial (transitional cell) histology 1 ; AND
- o BCG unresponsive defined by one of the following:
- Persistent or recurrent CIS within 12 months of receiving adequate BCG 2 ; OR
- Recurrent high-grade Ta/T1 disease within 6 months of receiving adequate BCG 3 ; OR
- T1 high-grade disease at the first evaluation following complete induction BCG course alone (5 of 6 doses of an initial induction course); AND
- Prescribed by, or in consultation with, a urologist or oncologist
