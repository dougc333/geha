Anktiva (nogapendekin alfa inbakicept-pmln)
- Member is 18 years of age or older; AND
- Member will be receiving nogapendekin alfa inbakicep-pmln (Anktiva) in combination with BCG; AND
- Member has non-muscle invasive bladder cancer with carcinoma in situ (CIS) that has the following characteristics:
- o High-grade; AND
- o Urothelial (transitional cell) histology 1 ; AND
- o BCG unresponsive defined by one of the following:
- Persistent or recurrent CIS within 12 months of receiving adequate BCG 2 ; OR
- Recurrent high-grade Ta/T1 disease within 6 months of receiving adequate BCG 3 ; OR
- T1 high-grade disease at the first evaluation following complete induction BCG course alone (5 of 6 doses of an initial induction course); AND
- Prescribed by, or in consultation with, a urologist or oncologist

Inlexzo (gemcitabine)
- Member is 18 years of age or older; AND
- Member has BCG-unresponsive non-muscle invasive bladder cancer with carcinoma in situ (CIS), with or without papillary tumors that has the following characteristics:
- o High-grade; AND
- o Urothelial (transitional cell) histology AND
- o BCG unresponsive defined by one of the following:
- Persistent or recurrent CIS within 12 months of receiving adequate BCG 4 ; OR
- Recurrent high-grade Ta/T1 disease within 6 months of receiving adequate BCG 5 ; OR
- T1 high-grade disease at the first evaluation following complete induction BCG course alone (5 of 6 doses of an initial induction course); AND
- Prescribed by, or in consultation with, a urologist or oncologist

Universal Approval Criteria:
Unless otherwise noted, the review criteria used by OncoHealth to determine medical necessity for anticancer treatments and supportive agents include, but is not limited to:
- National Comprehensive Cancer Network® (NCCN) - use consistent with NCCN recommendations carrying a Category 1 or 2A level of evidence; OR
- United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA) labeling - new drugs or regimens (combinations of drugs) consistent with the product labeling; OR
- Indications not included in the official FDA labeling or recommended by NCCN (Category 1 or 2A level of evidence) may be considered if determined to be medically necessary per one or more of the following compendia:
- o Clinical Pharmacology (Strong For); OR
- o Wolters Kluwer Lexi-Drugs® (Level A); OR
1 Mixed histology tumors are supported if transitional cell histology is predominant
2 Adequate BCG trial is defined as at least 5 of 6 doses of an initial induction PLUS at least 2 doses as part of maintenance treatment or a second induction
3 Adequate BCG trial is defined as at least 5 of 6 doses of an initial induction PLUS at least 2 doses as part of maintenance treatment or a second induction
