Criteria applied to all requests:
- Hemoglobin values are obtained within 28 days of the date of administration; AND
- Iron stores must be adequate with a transferrin saturation (TSAT) ≥ 20% AND a ferritin ≥ 100 ng/mL within the last 120 days; AND
- Other causes of anemia (such as to folate deficiency, B-12 deficiency, iron deficiency, hemolysis, bleeding, bone marrow fibrosis etc.) must be ruled out
