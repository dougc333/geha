Authorizations are issued for 6 (six) months, unless the ordering physician requests a different timespan or the patients' unique circumstance or condition supports the medical necessity for a different authorization timeframe. Reauthorization requests are reviewed for efficacy, safety and tolerability.

Drug Preference, Prior Authorization, HCPCS Code and Description
Preference Requires Prior Auth Drug Name HCPCS Code Description
Preferred Yes Trodelvy J9317 Injection, sacituzumab govitecan-hziy, 2.5 mg
Non-preferred Yes Datroway J9011 Injection,datopotamab deruxtecan-dlnk, 1 mg
  • Members must have documentation of a contraindication, or intolerance, to Trodelvy or clinically appropriate rationale for treatment with Datroway for overlapping indications or compendium supported uses in hormone-receptor positive metastatic breast cancer.
  • Preferred product requirements apply to new starts only.

Indication Specific Criteria Breast cancer – unresectable or metastatic

  • The member is 18 years of age or older; AND
  • The member has an ECOG performance status of 0-11; AND
  • The member has hormone receptor (HR) positive disease; AND
  • The member has HER2 negative (IHC 0, IHC 1+ or IHC 2+/ISH-) disease; AND
  • Datroway is used as subsequent therapy after the following:
    • Endocrine Therapy; AND
    • Chemotherapy; AND
    • Enhertu (if member is eligible for treatment with Enhertu, as per current version of NCCN Guidelines)

1 0 to 1 - ECOG score of 2 or 3 will be considered on a case-by-case basis.
