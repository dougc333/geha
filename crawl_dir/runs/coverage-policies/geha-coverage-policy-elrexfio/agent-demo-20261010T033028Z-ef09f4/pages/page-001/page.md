Review Date: 2/16/2026

Policy type: Medical Necessity

Line of Business: All G.E.H.A Commercial Medical Plans
 

Authorizations are issued for 6 (six) months, unless the ordering physician requests a different timespan or the patients' unique circumstance or condition supports the medical necessity for a different authorization timeframe. Reauthorization requests are reviewed for efficacy, safety and tolerability.

## Indication Specific Criteria:

### Multiple Myeloma (MM)

- Member is 18 years of age or older; AND

- Member has received at least four prior therapies and is refractory to at least one proteasome inhibitor (PI), one immunomodulatory agent (IMiD), and one anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody; AND

- Member has an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) score of 0 to 2 1 ; AND

- Member has not had a stem cell transplant within 12 weeks; AND

- Member has not experienced disease progression while on a bispecific B-cell maturation antigen (BCMA) – directed CD3 T-cell engager – containing regimen; AND

- Must be prescribed by, or in consultation with, an oncologist

## Universal Approval Criteria:
