Clinical Assistance programs, Definition = For more information, visit geha.com/My-Member-Portal G.E.H.A offers several programs to support and partner with you during your healthcare journey. When you are experiencing a new diagnosis or struggling with managing your health, G.E.H.A is here to support you by offering various programs. You may find out more about our programs at geha.com/Your-Health-Journey. A nurse case manager may contact you to participate in one of these programs and provide assistance with the coordination of your care, education and clinical support. Please note that eligibility for these services may vary, and not all members will qualify.
