We cover donor testing for the actual solid organ donor or up to four bone marrow/stem cell transplant donors in addition to the testing of family members. Notes: • If you are a participant in a clinical trial, see Section 9, Clinical Trials , for coverage details. • The process for preauthorizing transplants is more extensive than the normal process.
