· Standard Option
- In-Network: The physician copays have changed for primary care, including mental health and substance abuse ($35 from $20), specialist and associated specialties and Urgent care ($50 from $35). The MinuteClinic ® copay is now $15 from $10.
- Out-of-Network: The Plan's out-of-network physician cost share remains subject to deductible and coinsurance.
Sex Trait-Modification: Medical, surgical and prescription drug services related to Sex-Trait Modification for diagnosed gender dysphoria, are no longer covered by the Plan. See Section 3, 5(b), and 5(f). The Plan will continue to provide benefits related to mental and behavioral health services. See Section 5(e).
Breast Screening: The age to obtain appropriate age and medical history breast screening has been lowered to 40-74. Previously, it was age 50-74.
Mental Health & Substance Abuse visit: The copay for the first follow-up visit with your Physician within 30 days of an Emergency Room visit or Inpatient confinement for a Mental Health or Substance Abuse disorder is waived.
Prescription Drug Coverage: The maximum you will pay for Preferred, Non-Preferred, and Specialty medications has changed.

· High Option:
- Generic mail order copay is $25 from $20 for up to a 90-day supply.
- Network Retail Preferred drug is 25% coinsurance up to $250 maximum from $150 for a 30-day supply. Network Preferred mail order for a 90-day supply is 25% coinsurance up to $500 maximum from $350.
- Network Retail Non-Preferred drug is 40% coinsurance up to $400 maximum from $200 for a 30-day supply. Network Non-Preferred mail order for a 90-day supply is 40% coinsurance up to $800 maximum from $500.
- Preferred Specialty medications are 25% up to $350 maximum from $150 and Non-Preferred Specialty medications are 40% up to a $400 maximum from $200 (typically a 30-day supply).

· Standard Option:
- Generic mail order copay is $25 from $20 for up to a 90-day supply.
- Network Retail Preferred drug is 40% coinsurance up to $350 maximum from $250 for a 30-day supply. Network Preferred mail order for a 90-day supply is 40% coinsurance up to $700 maximum from $550.
- Network Retail Non-Preferred drug is 60% coinsurance up to $550 maximum from $350 for a 30-day supply. Network Non-Preferred mail order for a 90-day supply is 60% coinsurance up to $1,100 maximum from $650.
- Preferred Specialty medications are 50% up to $450 maximum from $250 and Non-Preferred Specialty medications are 50% up to a $600 maximum from $400 (typically a 30-day supply).
See Section 5f, Pharmacy, for more details based on whether you get your prescription at a Retail or Mail Order pharmacy.
Urine Drug Screening (UDS): The test limit for Urinary Drug Screening has been removed. Previously, it was 16 tests per calendar year.

· High Option (only)
:
- In-network hospital stay is $200 copay per day, up to 5 days and coinsurance. Previously, it was $100 copay per admission, and coinsurance.
- Out-of-network hospital stay is $500 copay per day, up to 5 days and coinsurance. Previously, it was $300 copay per admission, and coinsurance.

· High Option:
- The Emergency Room cost share is 30% of the Plan allowance, after deductible. Previously, it was 15% of the Plan allowance, after deductible.
- The cost share for Emergency Room services related to an Accidental Injury continues to be waived when services are rendered within 48 hours of an Accident. Previously, it was 72 hours of an Accident.

· Standard Option:
- The Emergency Room cost share is 35% of the Plan allowance, after deductible. Previously, it was 20% of the Plan allowance, after deductible.

· High Option:
- The in-network coinsurance amount is 20% of the Plan allowance, after deductible. Previously it was 10% of the Plan allowance, after deductible.
- The out-of-network coinsurance amount is 50%, after deductible. Previously it was 35% of the Plan allowance, after deductible.
