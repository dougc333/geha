Treatment therapies (cont.) HDHP

Physical, occupational, speech, habilitative, and rehabilitative therapy HDHP
We cover up to 60 outpatient therapy visits per person per calendar year for the combined services when a physician: • orders the care; • identifies the specific professional skills the patient requires and the medical necessity for skilled services; and • indicates the length of time the services are needed. Notes: • Inpatient therapy services are not applied to the visit limits, see Section 5(c), Inpatient hospital • Rehabilitation and Habilitative therapy, see Section 10, Definitions of Terms In-network: 5% of the Plan allowance Out-of-network: 35% of the Plan allowance and any difference between our allowance and the billed amount
Not covered: • Exercise programs • Long-term rehabilitative therapy • Maintenance therapy-measurable improvement is not expected, or progress is no longer demonstrated • Hot and cold packs • Hippotherapy • Rehabilitative services intended to teach or enhance Instrumental Activities of Daily Living (therapy to promote skills associated with independent living, such as shopping, using a phone, cleaning, laundry, preparing meals, managing medications, driving, or managing money/finances) • Sensory, Auditory, or Sensory Integration Therapy • Biofeedback, educational, recreational or milieu therapy All charges
