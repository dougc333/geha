Clinical Assistance programs G.E.H.A offers several programs to support and partner with you during your healthcare journey. When you are experiencing a new diagnosis or struggling with managing your health, G.E.H.A is here to support you by offering various programs. You may find out more about our programs at geha.com/Your-Health-Journey. A nurse case manager may contact you to participate in one of these programs and provide assistance with the coordination of your care, education and clinical support. Please note that eligibility for these services may vary, and not all members will qualify.
Health Education resources Visit our website at geha.com/Provider-Partner-Tools for information on: • General health topics • Links to healthcare news • Cancer and other specific diseases • Drugs/medication interactions • Kids' health • Patient safety information
Care Support • Helpful website links • G.E.H.A has a strong patient safety program. Pharmacy initiatives help ensure that members have fewer health complications related to prescription drugs. Disease management programs help our members with specific health conditions such as heart disease and diabetes. Medical case managers assist patients with high-risk pregnancies, durable medical equipment, transplants and other special needs. • Patient safety information is available online at geha.com/Provider-Partner-Tools.
Account Management tools If you have an HDHP (with either the HSA or HRA): • Complete claims payment history is available online through geha.com/My-Member- Portal; and • You will also receive an (EOB) after every claim. If you have an HSA under the HDHP: • You will receive a monthly statement from the HSA Bank™ outlining your HSA account balance and activity for the month • You may also access your HSA account on-line at geha.com/HDHP-HSA. If you have an HRA under the HDHP: • Your HRA account balance will be available on-line through your Member Account at geha.com/My-Member-Portal; and • To request reimbursement from your HRA account for qualified out-of-pocket medical expenses that are not submitted to G.E.H.A by your doctor, hospital, dentist or pharmacy, call G.E.H.A's Customer Care at 800-821-6136 or go to geha.com/My- Member-Portal to obtain our Health Reimbursement Arrangement Claim Form. This form is also used to request reimbursement from your HRA for Medicare premiums.
