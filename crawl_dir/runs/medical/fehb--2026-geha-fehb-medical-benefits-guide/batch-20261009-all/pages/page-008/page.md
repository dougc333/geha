HDHP

Plan features:
+ A popular HDHP plan chosen by federal employees
+ Get up to $2,000 from G.E.H.A into a health savings account (HSA) to use for qualified health care expenses now or in the future 1
+ Contribute your own dollars to the account tax-free and lower your yearly taxable income 2
+ Choose to invest your HSA savings and watch it grow tax-free as your money carries over year-to-year 2
+ Plus, get a $100 contribution when you open a new HSA account
+ Pay no more than 5% out-of-pocket on all medical services after deductible
+ Included vision at no additional cost
