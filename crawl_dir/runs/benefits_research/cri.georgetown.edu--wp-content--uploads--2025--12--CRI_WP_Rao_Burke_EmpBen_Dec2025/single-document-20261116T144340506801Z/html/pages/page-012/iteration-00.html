Figure 4 documents differences over time in access to employer-sponsored life insurance. The findings show sizeable gaps in access between non-Hispanic white adults and the other aggregated groups that increased over the window of analysis, from 6 percentage points in 2021, to 11 percentage points in 2023 (both differences are statistically significant). Male-female differences are relatively constant – with men reporting higher levels of access by about 4 – 6 percentage points over the period (all differences are statistically significant).
