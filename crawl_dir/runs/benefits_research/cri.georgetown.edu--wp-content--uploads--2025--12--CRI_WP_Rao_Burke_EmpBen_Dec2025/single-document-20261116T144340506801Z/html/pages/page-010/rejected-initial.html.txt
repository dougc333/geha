<p>Figure 2 examines disparities in access to employer-sponsored health insurance. Findings show consistent, and growing, gaps in access between non-white Hispanic adults and those in other racial and ethnic groups. In 2021, non-Hispanic white adults were 2 percentage points more likely to report access than those in other groups (not statistically significant), yet this gap grew to 5 percentage points at the end of the sample in 2023 (statistically significant). Conversely, findings show relatively large differences in access between men and women that close over time. In 2021 men were 10 percentage points more likely to report their employer offers health insurance, while this gap reduced in half by 2023 to 5 percentage points (both differences are statistically significant).</p>
