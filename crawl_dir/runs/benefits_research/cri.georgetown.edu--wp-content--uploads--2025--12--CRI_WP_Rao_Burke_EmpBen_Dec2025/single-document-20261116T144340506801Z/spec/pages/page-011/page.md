Figure 3 explores differences in access to employer-sponsored defined contribution accounts. Here findings show relatively temporally stable, and large, racial and ethnic differences – non-Hispanic white adults are 6 – 8 percentage points more likely to report access than the other aggregated groups in each year (all differences are statistically significant). Similar to differences between genders in access to employer sponsored health insurance, initially large male-female differences in access to employer-sponsored retirement accounts reduce somewhat over time, but do not disappear; men are 9 percentage points more likely than women to report access to a retirement plan at their workplace in 2021, yet this difference reduces to 4 percentage points in 2023 (both differences are statistically significant).
