Figure 5 examines disparities in access to employer-sponsored disability insurance. Here the findings show relatively substantial, and persistent, gaps in access across race/ethnicity and sex. Non-Hispanic white adults are 7 – 8 percentage points more likely to report access than the other groups in aggregate, while men are 5 – 6 percentage points more likely to report access than women across years (all differences are statistically significant).
