Outsourcing benefits: Today, employers that manage their leave and absence management programs in-house say they do so because it is less expensive (84%), gives them more control (65%) and supports a better employee experience (59%). But as employers face increased complexity and carriers invest in cost-effective and high-impact experiences, we expect to see a rise in outsourced administration of these programs. See Figure 12. Firms that are interested in outsourcing see the greatest benefits in navigating the regulatory environment and offering integrated experiences. But carriers must be ready to demonstrate value as more brokers and employers adopt a "show-me" mindset in evaluating their options for administering leave management benefits.
