Planning digital enhancements: In addition to pressure from employers and brokers, further enhancements to digital experiences are necessary to satisfy rising employee expectations and new work models. Indeed, employee interest in digital capabilities has widely increased since our 2023 study, with online enrollment and claims submission topping the list, likely driven by continued growth of younger generations in the workforce. Mobile apps and text messaging are particularly appealing to younger workers. See Figure 15.
