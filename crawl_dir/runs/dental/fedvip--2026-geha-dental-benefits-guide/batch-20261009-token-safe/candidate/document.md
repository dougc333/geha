## ORAL HEALTH EQUALS OVERALL HEALTH

Brush up on the value of supplemental dental coverage.

Even	without	major	dental	events,	yearly	cleanings	add	up when	you	pay	out-of-pocket.	Choosing	an	affordable	dental plan	from	G.E.H.A	ensures	you're	covered	for	yearly	visits	or the	unexpected	like	a	toothache	or	dental	emergencies.

Oral	health	is	important	to	our	overall	health.	In	fact,	people who	have	preventive	cleanings	may	be	at	lower	risk	for chronic disease including heart disease and some forms of cancer, according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention	(CDC).
