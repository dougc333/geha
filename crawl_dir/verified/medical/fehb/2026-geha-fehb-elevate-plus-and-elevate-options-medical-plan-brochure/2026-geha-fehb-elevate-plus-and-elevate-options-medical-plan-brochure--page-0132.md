## 2026 Rate Information for Government Employees Health Association, Inc. Benefit Plan

To compare your FEHB health plan options, please go to www. opm.gov/fehbcompare.

To review premium rates for all FEHB health plan options please go to www. opm.gov/FEHBpremiums or www. opm.gov/Tribalpremium .

Premiums for Tribal employees are shown under the Monthly Premium Rate column. The amount shown under employee pay is the maximum you will pay. Your Tribal employer may choose to pay a higher portion of your premium. Please contact your Tribal Benefits Officer for exact rates.
