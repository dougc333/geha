Resources

G.E.H.A has a strong patient safety program. Pharmacy initiatives help ensure that members have

fewer health complications related to prescription drugs. Disease management programs help our

members with specific health conditions such as heart disease and diabetes. Medical case managers

assist patients with high-risk pregnancies, durable medical equipment, transplants and other special

needs. Patient safety information is available online at geha.com/Provider-Partner-Tools.
