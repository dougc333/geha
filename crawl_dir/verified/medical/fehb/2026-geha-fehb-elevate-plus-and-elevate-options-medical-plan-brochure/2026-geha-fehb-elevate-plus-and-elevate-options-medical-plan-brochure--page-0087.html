Contraceptive coverage is available at no cost to FEHB

members. The contraceptive benefit includes at least one

option in each of the HRSA-supported categories of

contraception (as well as the screening, education,

counseling, and follow-up care). Over the counter

(prescription required) and prescription drugs approved

by the FDA to prevent an unintended pregnancy are

included.

Any contraceptive that is not already available without

cost sharing on the formulary can be accessed through

the contraceptive exceptions process described on our

website at geha.com/Family-Planning-Options or by

calling CVS Caremark at 844-443-4279. Exception

requests for contraceptive coverage will be processed

within 24 hours of receiving complete information.

Reimbursement for over-the-counter contraceptives

(prescription required) can be submitted by sending in

your original prescription receipt obtained from your

pharmacy to:
