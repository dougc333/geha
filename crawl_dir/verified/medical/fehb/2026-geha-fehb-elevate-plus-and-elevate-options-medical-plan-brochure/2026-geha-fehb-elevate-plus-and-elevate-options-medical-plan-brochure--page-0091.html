covered medication

• Medical devices, or supplies such as dressings and

• Drugs prescribed in connection with Sex-Trait

Modification for treatment of gender dysphoria

If you are mid-treatment under this Plan, within a surgical

or chemical regimen for Sex-Trait Modification for

diagnosed gender dysphoria, for services for which you

received coverage under the 2025 Plan brochure, you may

seek an exception to continue care for that treatment.

Ongoing sex-trait modification hormone treatment is not

a covered benefit under this plan as of 1/1/2026. Our

exception process is as follows: To initiate the exception

process, contact Customer Care at 800-821-6136,

Monday-Friday, 8 a.m. - 8 p.m. E.T. If you disagree with

our decision on your exception, please see Section 8 of

this brochure for the disputed claims process.

Individuals under age 19 are not eligible for exceptions

related to services for ongoing surgical or hormonal

treatment for diagnosed gender dysphoria.
