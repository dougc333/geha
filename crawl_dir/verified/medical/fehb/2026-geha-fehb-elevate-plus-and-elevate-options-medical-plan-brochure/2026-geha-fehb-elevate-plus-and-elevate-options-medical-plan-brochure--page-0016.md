## Section 2. Changes for 2026

Do not rely only on these change descriptions; this Section is not an official statement of benefits. For that, go to Section 5, Benefits. Also, we edited and clarified language throughout the brochure; any language change not shown here is a clarification that does not change benefits.

## Changes to Elevate Option

- Premium: Your share of the premium rate will increase for Self Only, Self Plus One and Self and Family. See back cover.
- Deductible: The Plan will increase the calendar year deductible for in-network services for Self Only to $750 from $500 and for Self Plus One and Self and Family to $1,500 from $1,000. For out-of-network services, the calendar year deductible will increase for Self Only to $2,000 from $1,000 and for Self Plus One and Self and Family to $4,000 from $2,000.
- Out-of-Pocket-Maximum: The Plan will increase the in-network catastrophic out-of-pocket maximum for Self Only to $10,600 from $8,500 and for Self Plus One and Self Plus One and Self and Family to $21,200 from $17,000.
- Emergency Room: The Plan will increase the emergency room cost share to 35% of the Plan allowance, after the deductible from 25% of the Plan allowance, after the deductible for in and out-of-network services.
- Prescription Drugs: The Plan will increase the cost share network retail generic drugs to a $10 copay from a $4 copay (up to a 30-day supply), to $20 from $8 (31 to 60-day supply), and to $30 from $12 (61 to 90-day supply). See Section 5(f), Prescription Drug Benefits, page 83.

## Changes to Elevate Plus Option

- Premium: Your share of the premium rate will increase for Self Only, Self Plus One and Self and Family. See back cover.
- Coinsurance: The Plan will increase the in-network coinsurance amount to 20% of the Plan allowance, after the deductible. from 15% of the Plan allowance, after the deductible.
- Emergency Room: The Plan will increase the emergency room cost share to 25% of the Plan allowance, after the deductible from 15% of the Plan allowance, after the deductible for in and out-of-network services.
- Prescription Drugs: The Plan will increase the cost share for network retail generic drugs to a $15 copay from a $10 copay (up to a 30-day supply), to $30 from $20 (31 to 60-day supply), and to $45 from $30 (61 to 90-day supply); network retail preferred drugs to a $100 copay from an $80 copay (up to a 30-day supply), to a $200 copay from a $160 copay (31 to 60-day supply), and to a $300 copay from a $240 copay (61 to 90-day supply). In addition, the cost share maximum for specialty CVS pharmacy generic and preferred drugs will increase from 40% of Plan allowance to $700 from $500 (up to a 30-day supply), to $1,400 from $1,000 (31 to 60-day supply) and to $2,100 maximum from $1,500 (61 to 90-day supply). See Section 5(f), Prescription Drug Benefits, page 83.

## Changes to both Elevate and Elevate Plus Options

- Breast Cancer Screening: The Plan will expand preventive breast cancer screening from ages 50-74 to age 40-74. See Section 5(a), Preventive care/adult, page 37.
- Mental Health and Substance Abuse: The Plan will expand coverage to include no cost share on one primary care physician or specialist visit for the management of a mental health condition as a follow up within 30 days of an emergency room visit for mental health and substance use disorder. Previously, there was no cost share for the first follow-up PCP or specialist visit within 30 days of a mental health inpatient confinement only. See Section 5(e), Mental Health and Substance Use Disorder Benefits, page 78.
- Urine Drug Screening: The Plan will remove limits on urine drug testing. Previously, it was covered up to 16 tests for urine drug testing per person per calendar year. See Section 5(a), Lab, x-ray, and other diagnostic tests, page 35.
- Reconstructive Surgery: The Plan will remove coverage for surgery for Sex-Trait Modification to treat gender dysphoria. See Section 5(a), Reconstructive surgery, page 57.
- Prescription Drugs: The Plan will remove coverage for drugs prescribed in connection with sex-trait modification for treatment of gender dysphoria. See Section 5(f), Non-covered medications and supplies, page 94.
