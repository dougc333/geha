(https://pdh62b4ddxm6mw7aokdbtwream0nupph.lambda-url.us-west-2.on.aws/chat).

- **Text query**: asks what the figure shows; the caption answers it. The figure
  should come back as a source, with its thumbnail under the answer.
- **Figure query**: asks for a detail visible only in the image; the expected
  answer is next to it.

Queries and expected answers were generated by Nova Lite from each image and
caption, and **are not hand-checked**: an expected answer can be a misreading,
so compare it with the image. Thumbnails link to the local PNGs in
