GEHA Secondary Conditions and Associated Policies - QC Inventory

Purpose
-------
This file records secondary conditions, treatment-related complications, and
disease-associated manifestations verified in the source policy PDFs. It is a
manual QC artifact and is not an assertion of guaranteed member coverage.
Medical necessity criteria and member-specific benefit documents still apply.

7. Management of Checkpoint Inhibitor Related Toxicities
   Policy: Intravenous Immune Globulin
   Source: geha-coverage-policy-intravenous-immune-globulin.pdf
   Documented manifestations:
   - Myocarditis not improved after pulse-dose methylprednisolone
   - Severe or life-threatening bullous dermatitis
   - Stevens-Johnson Syndrome
   - Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis
   - Moderate or severe steroid-refractory myalgias or myositis
   - Severe myasthenia gravis
   - Moderate or severe Guillain-Barre Syndrome
   - Severe peripheral neuropathy
   - Severe encephalitis
   - Transverse myelitis
   - Moderate or severe pneumonitis not improved after corticosteroids

8. Management of CAR-T Cell Related Toxicities
   Policy: Intravenous Immune Globulin
   Source: geha-coverage-policy-intravenous-immune-globulin.pdf
   Documented manifestations:
   - Secondary hypogammaglobulinemia with serious or recurrent bacterial infections
   - Cytokine release syndrome refractory to high-dose corticosteroids and anti-IL-6 therapy

9. Iatrogenic infertility
   Policy: Infertility Services
   Source: geha-coverage-policy-infertility-services.pdf
   Documented causes:
   - Surgery
   - Radiation
   - Chemotherapy
   - Other medical treatment affecting reproductive organs
   - Gonadotoxic therapies
   - Ovary or testicle removal for treatment of disease

Not explicitly supported as a condition
---------------------------------------

    Status: Not verified as an explicit condition in the downloaded policies.
    Related documents:
    - geha-coverage-policy-short-acting-gcsfs.pdf

QC boundary
-----------
- Items 1-10 are explicit policy indications or explicitly named treatment-related categories.
- Items 11-13 are manifestations found inside broader disease approval criteria.
- Item 14 is intentionally excluded from the verified condition set.
- These mappings do not guarantee coverage, authorization, or payment.
