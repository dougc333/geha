## **6. THERAPEUTIC DUPLICATION**

Patients are limited to **one** long-acting and **one** short-acting CNS stimulant at a time within the quantity/dosing limits. Overrides may be approved:

For the date of service when patients are switching from one agent to another.
For 1 year when the member requires two different strengths of one medication because the prescribed dosage cannot be achieved otherwise (e.g., Concerta 18 mg and Concerta 27 mg taken together once daily).
