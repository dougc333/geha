## **CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM: ANTI-MIGRAINE AGENTS, CGRP INHIBITORS**

### **GUIDELINES FOR USE**

CGRP medications are limited to two brand switches per class (acute and preventative) per year.
CGRP medications are limited to one for acute and preventive migraine concurrently.

**Approval Duration: 1 year, unless otherwise specified**
