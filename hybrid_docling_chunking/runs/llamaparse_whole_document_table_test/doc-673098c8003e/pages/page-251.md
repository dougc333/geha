## **BLOOD MODIFIERS: ERYTHROPOIESIS STIMULATING AGENTS**

### **GUIDELINES FOR USE**

**Approval Duration: 3 months initial; 1 year renewal, unless otherwise specified**

#### **1. PREFERRED WITH PA (PDP) CRITERIA**

<tr><td>Aranesp <sup>CC</sup></td><td>● Member is NOT receiving hemodialysis*; <strong>AND</strong><br />● Documentation (e.g., progress note, laboratory report) of hemoglobin (Hgb) &#x3C; 10 g/dL in the past 90 days; <strong>AND</strong><br />● Prescribed for one of the following diagnoses:<br />○ Anemia associated with chronic renal failure; <strong>OR</strong><br />○ Anemia associated with kidney transplantation; <strong>OR</strong><br />○ Treatment of chemotherapy-induced anemia for non-myeloid malignancies.<br /><br /><strong>Renewal Criteria:</strong><br />● Documentation (e.g., progress note, laboratory report) of response to therapy.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Retacrit <sup>CC</sup><br />Epogen <sup>CC</sup></td><td>● Member is NOT receiving hemodialysis*; <strong>AND</strong><br />● Documentation (e.g., progress note, laboratory report) of hemoglobin (Hgb) &#x3C; 10 g/dL in the past 90 days; <strong>AND</strong><br />● Prescribed for one of the following diagnoses:<br />○ Anemia associated with chronic renal failure; <strong>OR</strong><br />○ Anemia associated with kidney transplantation; <strong>OR</strong><br />○ Treatment of chemotherapy-induced anemia for non-myeloid malignancies; <strong>OR</strong><br />○ Drug-induced anemia (e.g., Retrovir, Combivir or ribavirin); <strong>OR</strong><br />○ Autologous blood donations by patients scheduled to undergo nonvascular surgery.<br /><br /><strong>Renewal Criteria:</strong><br />● Documentation (e.g., progress note, laboratory report) of response to therapy.</td></tr>
